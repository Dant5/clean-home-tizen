CLEAN HOME SETUP · __PLATFORM__

Your Home button.
Your home screen.

Install Clean Home, then make it appear when you press Home or switch on your TV.

No root. Your apps stay installed. Samsung Home stays available.

Experimental Home replacement: currently accepts QA55Q60BAWXXY / T-NKLBUABC-1720.7 only. Check the full model and software version in your TV’s About screen. Other Samsung models can run Check TV and send compatibility results in step 3. Their Home settings stay unchanged.

1. Get your TV ready

Use a computer on the same home network as your TV.

On the TV, open Apps and enter 1 2 3 4 5. Turn Developer Mode On, enter your computer’s local IP address as Host PC IP, and restart as prompted.

Find your computer’s address / setup help

2. Install the TV app

Open Apps2Samsung, select your TV, then choose Install a file….

Choose this file from the same folder as this guide:

__WIDGET__

Follow the Samsung sign-in and certificate prompts using your own account. For an update, reuse your existing signing profile.

Once installed, open Clean Home from the TV’s Apps screen.

3. Make it your Home screen

Close Apps2Samsung and keep the TV on. In the extracted setup folder, open __START_TOOL____RUNTIME__.

In the setup page, enter the TV’s IP address from its Network Status → IP Settings screen. Choose Check TV, read the confirmation and choose Use Clean Home.

Check TV runs independent capability checks, including on unlisted Samsung models. If Home setup is unavailable, review the results and choose Send compatibility report. You can also save a local copy. Original Home/startup preferences are saved before any supported Home change.

Check it: press Home on the physical remote. Then turn the TV off normally and back on. Clean Home should appear. Its Samsung Home button still opens Samsung’s interface.

Keep the recovery folder shown by the tool. Use Undo setup before uninstalling Clean Home. Help and Undo

4. Make it yours

Choose +, then an installed app. Use the remote’s arrows to move and centre button to select. Hold Select for about a second to change a shortcut.

You can now close the setup tool. No PC needs to stay running.

The TV app and Home tool are together in this download; Samsung signing still uses Apps2Samsung. No settings change happens just by opening this guide. Project and credits
